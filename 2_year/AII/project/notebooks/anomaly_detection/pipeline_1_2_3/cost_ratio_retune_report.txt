roughly 3x (16630->5123 fair, 11317->4926 plain) by removing the inflation an
uncalibrated score was adding to the missed-conversion term. Raising the ratio
recovers catch rate by spending some of that savings back -- deliberately, on
a score that is now actually trustworthy, rather than by accident on one that
wasn't. The DIDI column shows whether the fairness benefit survives re-tuning.
