-- Part 3: does the fairness correction change WHY the model predicts what it
   predicts, or only WHAT number comes out? --
fairness_correction.py applies group_shift(attr, value) as a constant additive
term computed on train and applied on test -- it does not touch the underlying
RandomForest or its SHAP attributions at all. So by construction: every patient
in the same (protected-attribute-value) group receives the identical shift
regardless of which features drove their original score. The per-patient SHAP
decomposition above (feature-level, individual) is UNCHANGED by the correction;
only the final scalar RISK_SCORE moves. This is an honest limitation worth
stating directly if asked: the correction repairs the group-level statistic
DIDI is defined on, but does not remove whatever proxy signal (Part 2) still
drives individual disagreement within a group. The Lagrangian in-training fix
(fairness_correction_tf.py) is the one that could, in principle, change the
feature-level story -- but it was already shown to trade away too much AUC
(0.791->0.570) to be worth that theoretical advantage here.
