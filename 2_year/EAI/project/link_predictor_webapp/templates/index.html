Hidden gems

Not recent by today's date, and low citation count, but the model still flags them as relevant — the papers the Matthew effect leaves behind.

Model: trained GraphSAGE link predictor (GNNs/weights/graphsage_link_predictor_es_ap_final.pt). Explanations: a CART decision-tree surrogate (Symbolic Knowledge Extraction) trained offline to approximate the model, falling back to a live per-instance LIME explanation when the tree doesn't fit a specific prediction well. Data built offline by build_recommender_index.py. Runs entirely locally — no data leaves this machine.
