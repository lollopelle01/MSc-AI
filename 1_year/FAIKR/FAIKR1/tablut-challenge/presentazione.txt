While we were implementing our player we faced several challenges.

For example we had problems while we were debugging our Node class, since we naively
applied it to the Tablut domain without considering the dimension of the problem, so we 
implemented an easier one to get that done easily.

Then, we noticed that our alpha-beta cut was considering more a deep evaluation wrt the same
but at a lower depth so as a first attempt we tried to weighten the depth but it wasn't
enough effective and we were late so we patched it by applying a breadth-first to the first layer.

In the end we had several problems in comparing different heuristics and finding a good tradeoff
between simplicity and accuracy, so we implemented a parser that could obtain a dataset out of the 
log files of the server in order to extract the moves and the results of each game.
Moreover we implemented a games executor that could simulate the tablut-challenge and we merged those
applications to compare 4 of the different heuristics we wrote.
