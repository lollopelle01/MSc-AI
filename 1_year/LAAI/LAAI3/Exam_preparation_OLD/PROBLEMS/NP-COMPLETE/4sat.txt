4SAT = {F in 4CNF | F is satisfiable}

Prove that 4SAT is NP-complete.

============================================================

OUTPUT: L=[(l_1,v_1), ..., (l_N,v_N)]

• Certificate: literal assignment dict L=[l_1:v_1, ..., l_N:v_N]
