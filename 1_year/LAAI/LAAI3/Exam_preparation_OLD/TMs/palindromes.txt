Write a 2-tape TM which accepts palindromes of {0,1}*

STATES
q_copy = copies 1° tape in 2°
q_back = 1° tape goes to the beginning
q_comp = compares from start of 1° tape and from end of 2°

TRANSITION FUNCTION
(q_init, (>,>)) --> (q_copy, (>,>), (R,R))

(q_copy, (0,_)) --> (q_copy, (0,0), (R,R))
(q_copy, (1,_)) --> (q_copy, (1,1), (R,R))
(q_copy, (_,_)) --> (q_back, (_,_), (L,L))

(q_back, (>,-)) --> (q_comp, (>,-), (R,S))	
# NB: order is important! In this way > is excluded from the "-" of the next rule
(q_back, (-,-)) --> (q_back, (-,-), (L,S))	

(q_comp, (0,0)) --> (q_comp, (0,0), (R,L))
(q_comp, (1,1)) --> (q_comp, (1,1), (R,L))
(q_comp, (0,1)) --> (q_fail, (0,1), (S,S))
(q_comp, (1,0)) --> (q_fail, (1,0), (S,S))
(q_comp, (_,>)) --> (q_succ, (_,>), (S,S))
