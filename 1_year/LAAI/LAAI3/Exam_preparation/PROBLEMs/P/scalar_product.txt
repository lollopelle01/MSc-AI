INPUT: L=[l_1, ..., l_n], P=[p_1, ..., p_n]
OUTPUT: sum(l_i*p_i)

PSEUDOCODE:
sum=0
for i in {1...n}:
     sum += L[i]*P[i]
return sum

1) The input can be encoded as lists of binary numbers, since rational numbers can be encoded as couple of integers (numerator,denominator)
2) The pseudocode works in O(n)
3) Intermediate results are polynomially bounded to the input as i and sum are finite numbers.
4) The intermediate operations are polytime.

The function is in FP.
