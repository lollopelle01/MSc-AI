The "Principia Mathematica" is a book by philosopher Bertrand Russel, published between 1910 and 1913, in which many theorems about mathematics are stated and proved to hold. Let PP be the set of theorems in "Principia Mathematica", appropriately encoded in a succinct way as finite binary strings. Which complexity class does PP belong to? Prove your claim.

Let T be the set of theorems in Principia Mathematica. Then, we can model PP as the set of (encoding) of pairs (t,T) where t is a theorem in T:

    PP = {⌞(t,T)⌟ | t ∈ T}.

A TM that receives in input the pair (t,T) and checks whether t ∈ T trivially works in poly-time in |⌞(t,T)⌟|, since both t and T belong to the input itself. Therefore, PP is in P.
